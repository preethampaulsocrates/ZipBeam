Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 19, 2026

SendThem ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding it.

1. What We Collect

  • Account information — if you sign up, we store your name, email address, and a securely hashed password (never the plain password).
  • Files you transfer — files sent through SendThem are temporarily stored on our server to relay them from your phone to your computer.
  • Usage data — basic analytics such as page visits, browser type, and approximate location (via Google Analytics, if enabled).

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To operate the file transfer service (creating sessions, relaying files).
  • To maintain your account and credit balance, if you've signed up.
  • To improve the product based on usage patterns.
  • To show advertisements that help keep SendThem free.

3. File Storage & Deletion

Files you transfer are automatically deleted when your session expires (within 30 minutes) or once downloaded, whichever applies. We do not access, view, or share the contents of your files.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited data with service providers strictly necessary to run SendThem (e.g. hosting providers, analytics, ad networks like Google AdSense).

5. Cookies

We use cookies to keep you signed in and to measure site usage. You can disable cookies in your browser, though this may affect login functionality.

6. Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at the email below.

7. Children's Privacy

SendThem is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Continued use of SendThem after changes means you accept the updated policy.

9. Contact Us

For any privacy questions or requests, email us at preethampaulsocrates@hotmail.com.
