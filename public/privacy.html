Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 2026

Welcome to ZipBeam. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use our file-sharing service.

Information We Collect

ZipBeam is designed to transfer files securely between devices with minimal data collection.

We may collect:

  • Temporary file data uploaded for transfer
  • Device and browser information
  • IP address for security and abuse prevention
  • Session identifiers required for file transfers

How We Use Information

We use collected information to:

  • Facilitate file transfers
  • Improve website performance
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Maintain service reliability and security

File Storage

Files uploaded through ZipBeam are stored temporarily.

Once a file has been successfully downloaded, it is automatically deleted from our servers. Expired transfer sessions and associated files are also removed automatically.

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect uploaded files and user sessions. However, no method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Third-Party Services

We may use trusted third-party providers for hosting, analytics, storage, and performance monitoring. These providers process data only as necessary to deliver the service.

Cookies

ZipBeam may use essential cookies to maintain sessions, improve functionality, and enhance user experience.

Children's Privacy

ZipBeam is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us through the Contact Us page.
