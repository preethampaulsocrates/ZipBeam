Frequently Asked Questions

How do I send files from my mobile to my laptop or PC?

Open ZipBeam on your laptop or PC browser, scan the QR code shown on screen using your mobile phone's camera, choose the files you want to send, and tap Send. The files appear instantly on your computer — no app, cable, or login required.

Can I transfer files from phone to PC without a USB cable?

Yes. ZipBeam lets you transfer files wirelessly from your phone to your PC or laptop using only a QR code scan and an internet connection — no USB cable needed.

Is ZipBeam free to use?

Yes, ZipBeam is completely free to use for sending files from your mobile phone to your computer or laptop.

Do I need to install an app to use ZipBeam?

No installation is required. ZipBeam works directly in your mobile browser and your computer's browser.

Is there any website to transfer files from mobile to laptop for free?

Yes — ZipBeam is a free website for transferring files from mobile to laptop. Just open zipbeam.in on your laptop, scan the QR code with your phone, and send your files instantly. No signup, no app, and no cost.

What is the fastest way to send photos from my phone to my computer?

The fastest way is to use ZipBeam: open it on your computer, scan the QR code with your phone's camera, select your photos, and send. It works over Wi-Fi or mobile data with no cables or apps.

How can I transfer files between phone and PC on the same Wi-Fi without an app?

ZipBeam works entirely in the browser, so there's nothing to install. Connect both devices to the internet, open zipbeam.in on your PC, scan the QR code from your phone, and your files transfer directly — Wi-Fi or mobile data both work.
