How to Send Files from Phone to Laptop Without a USB Cable

Published June 2026 · 3 min read

Lost your USB cable, or just don't want to dig it out of a drawer? You don't need one anymore. Here's exactly how to move photos, videos, and documents from your phone to your laptop or PC using nothing but a browser and a QR code.

Step 1: Open ZipBeam on your laptop or PC

Go to zipbeam.in in any browser on your computer. A QR code appears on screen immediately — no signup or download required.

Step 2: Scan the QR code with your phone

Open your phone's camera app (or any QR scanner) and point it at the code on your laptop screen. This opens a page on your phone that's instantly linked to your computer's session.

Step 3: Choose your files and send

Select the photos, videos, or documents you want to transfer, then tap Send. They appear on your laptop screen within seconds, ready to save.

Why this works better than a USB cable

  • No drivers or compatibility issues — works the same on Windows, Mac, Android, and iPhone since everything runs in the browser.
  • No physical cable needed — works over Wi-Fi or mobile data.
  • Faster for one-off transfers — no need to mount your phone as a drive or wait for a connection prompt.
