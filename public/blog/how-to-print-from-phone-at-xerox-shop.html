How to Print Documents from Your Phone at a Xerox / Print Shop

Published July 2026 · 3 min read

You're standing at a print shop with a document saved only on your phone — no pen drive, no laptop, and emailing it to yourself feels slow when the shop is busy. Here's the quickest way to get a file from your phone onto the shop's computer for printing.

The old ways, and why they're slow

  • Emailing yourself the file: works, but requires opening Gmail on the shop's PC, logging in (awkward on a public computer), finding the email, and downloading the attachment.
  • Bluetooth transfer: most shop PCs don't have Bluetooth turned on or paired, and pairing takes several minutes of back-and-forth.
  • WhatsApp Web: requires scanning a QR code to log into your own WhatsApp on their PC, which most people are uncomfortable doing on a shared computer.

The faster way: a QR code made for exactly this

Many print shops now display a small QR code at their counter — usually printed on ZipBeam, a free tool built for this. Here's how it works from your side as a customer:

  1. Scan the shop's QR code with your phone camera.
  2. A page opens on your phone. Tap "Send for Print."
  3. Select your document (PDF, Word file, image — anything).
  4. Tap send. The file appears instantly on the shop's screen, ready to print.

No login, no account, no app to install. The file only goes to print — it isn't saved anywhere permanently, so you don't need to worry about your personal documents lingering on a shared computer.

If you run a print shop

You can set up a permanent QR code with your own ZipBeam ID — print it once, stick it at your counter, and every customer who scans it can send you files directly. No re-printing a new code for each customer. Create your free ZipBeam account to get your own QR code.
